3 Networks you scan

ARPradar sends network traffic to the devices it scans. Use it only on networks you own or have permission to scan. You are responsible for how you use it.

4 Your data

ARPradar doesn't send your scans, settings or any other data to the author. What it finds stays on your computer unless you export or share it. Some features contact other services, only when you use them: Public IP (api.ipify.org), domain expiry (rdap.org), subdomain search (crt.sh, Cert Spotter), speed test (Apple's network-quality servers on the Mac, Cloudflare's speed-test servers on Windows), and your own UniFi controller for the network map. Their own terms apply to them.
